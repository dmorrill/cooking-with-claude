# White Sonora Double Pie Crust

**Source:** [Dry Storage Co.](https://drystorageco.com)
**Yield:** Double crust (top and bottom)
**Best For:** Pumpkin, chocolate silk, chess pies (custards)

---

## Why Sonora?

For ultra-tender, golden, delicate crusts.

**Why home bakers love Sonora:**
- Extra tender & flaky
- Light golden color
- Slightly sweet
- Great for custards

---

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient |
|--------|------------|
| 2 cups + 2 Tbsp | White Sonora Flour |
| 1 tsp | Sugar (can be omitted) |
| 1/2 tsp | Salt |
| 3/4 cup | Butter, cold |
| 8-12 Tbsp | Ice water or cold milk |

### Blend Alternatives

See [flour cheat sheet](../../ingredients/dry-storage-flour-cheat-sheet.md) for details.

- 100% Sonora
- 70/30 Sonora/Rouge
- 70/30 Sonora/AP

---

## Instructions

1. **Mix dry ingredients:** Mix together flour, sugar and salt.

2. **Cut in butter:** Cut butter into 1/2 inch cubes, add to the flour mix. Using fingers, squish each butter cube, until no cubes remain and butter resembles flakes.

3. **Add liquid:** Add cold water or milk, mix to combine until dough forms.

4. **First rest:** Cover, let rest for 5 minutes.

5. **First roll and fold:** Dust work surface with enough flour to keep dough from sticking. Roll dough into a rectangle 3 times as long as it is wide. Fold each third of the dough into itself, creating a square.

6. **Second roll and fold:** Roll dough again into a rectangle 3 times as long as it is wide. Repeat the same fold.

7. **Third roll and fold:** Roll dough one more time into a rectangle 3 times as long as it is wide, and repeat fold.

8. **Final roll and divide:** Roll dough into a rectangle 2 times as long as it is wide, divide dough into 2 equal portions.

9. **Shape and chill:** Shape into a disk, wrap in plastic wrap tightly, and place in the fridge for 2 hours.

10. **Before rolling:** Let dough sit out at room temperature for 30 minutes before rolling into crust.

---

## Notes

- Sonora absorbs more liquid - may need closer to 12 Tbsp water
- Softer handling - watch temperature and work quickly
- See [White Sonora flour guide](../../ingredients/white-sonora-flour.md) for high-altitude adjustments
