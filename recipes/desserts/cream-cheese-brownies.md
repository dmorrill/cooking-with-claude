# Cream Cheese Brownies

- **Source:** [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1025772-cream-cheese-brownies)
- **Total Time:** ~1 hour + cooling
- **Prep:** 10 minutes
- **Cook:** 24-28 minutes
- **Yield:** 16 servings
- **Tags:** dessert, brownies, valentine's, gifts

## Notes
- Great for making multiple batches as gifts
- Cream cheese should be soft but NOT warm — too warm = runny, no swirl
- Sugar may not fully dissolve after whisking — that's OK, proceed
- Don't let chocolate batter sit too long
- Can store at room temp in airtight container up to 3 days

## Cream Cheese Batter
- 8 oz cream cheese, softened
- 1 large egg
- ¼ cup (31g) powdered sugar
- 1 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

## Cocoa Batter
- 10 tbsp (141g) unsalted butter, plus more for pan
- 1 cup (200g) granulated sugar
- ⅓ cup (50g) semisweet chocolate chips
- 2 large eggs
- 1½ tsp vanilla extract
- ¾ cup (96g) all-purpose flour
- ¾ cup (71g) unsweetened cocoa powder (Dutch-processed or natural)
- ½ tsp baking powder
- ¾ tsp kosher salt (Diamond Crystal)

## Steps
1. Heat oven to 350°F. Butter and line 8-inch square pan with parchment (2-inch overhang on 2 sides for sling). Butter the parchment.
2. **Cream cheese batter:** Beat cream cheese, egg, powdered sugar, flour, and vanilla until combined.
3. **Cocoa batter:** In medium saucepan over medium heat, combine butter and sugar. Cook stirring until butter melts, remove from heat. Whisk vigorously until homogenous/opaque and sugar dissolved (~4 min). Add chocolate chips, let stand 1 min, whisk to combine.
4. Add eggs and vanilla, whisk smooth. Add flour, cocoa, baking powder, salt — stir to combine.
5. Transfer ~⅔ of cocoa batter to pan, spread even. Spread cream cheese batter over top. Dollop remaining ⅓ cocoa batter on top. Swirl with butter knife.
6. Bake 24-28 min until set and toothpick comes out with moist crumbs.
7. Cool completely on rack. Lift out with parchment sling, cut into squares.
