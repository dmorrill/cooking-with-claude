# Fantasy Fudge (Jet Puffed Marshmallow)
- **Prep Time**: 10 minutes
- **Cook Time**: 10-12 minutes
- **Chill Time**: 2-4 hours
- **Total Time**: 2.5-4.5 hours
- **Yield**: ~70 pieces (fills 13x9 pan)
- **Difficulty**: Medium (requires candy thermometer at altitude)
- **Type**: Christmas Dessert / Classic American
- **Source**: Jet Puffed / Kraft Marshmallow Creme jar

### Ingredients
- [ ] **Granulated sugar** (3 cups) - *Location: upstairs or downstairs pantry*
- [ ] **Salted butter** (3/4 cup / 1.5 sticks, room temp, cubed) - *Location: upstairs fridge*
- [ ] **Evaporated milk** (1 can, 5 oz, room temp) - *Location: downstairs pantry*
- [ ] **Semi-sweet chocolate chips** (1 bag, 12 oz, room temp) - *Location: downstairs or upstairs pantry*
- [ ] **Marshmallow creme** (1 jar, 7 oz, Jet Puffed) - *Location: pantry*
- [ ] **Vanilla extract** (1 tsp) - *Location: upstairs pantry*
- [ ] **Chopped walnuts** (1 cup, optional) - *Location: downstairs pantry*

### Equipment
- [ ] **13x9 baking pan**
- [ ] **Parchment paper**
- [ ] **Heavy-bottomed saucepan**
- [ ] **Candy thermometer** (essential at altitude!)
- [ ] **Spatula**
- [ ] **Timer**

### Instructions

#### 1. Prepare Pan
Line 13x9 pan with parchment paper, leaving overhang for easy removal

#### 2. Measure Everything First
Have all ingredients measured and near the stove - this moves fast once boiling starts!

#### 3. Cook the Base
1. Combine sugar, butter cubes, and evaporated milk in heavy-bottomed saucepan
2. Heat over medium, stirring constantly as butter melts and sugar dissolves
3. Bring to a rolling boil
4. **At altitude (5,280 ft): Cook to 224°F** (sea level: 234°F)
5. Or boil for approximately 4-4.5 minutes at altitude (5 min at sea level)
6. Remove from heat immediately when temperature is reached

#### 4. Add Remaining Ingredients
1. Add chocolate chips, stir until completely melted
2. Stir in marshmallow creme and vanilla, beat until combined
3. Fold in walnuts if using

#### 5. Pour and Chill
1. Pour into prepared pan
2. Smooth top with spatula
3. Cool completely at room temperature, then refrigerate until firm (2-4 hours)
4. Cut into 1-inch squares

### Notes

**Altitude Adjustment (tested 12/23/2025 at 5,280 ft):**
- Water boils at ~202°F at altitude instead of 212°F
- **Pull fudge at 224°F** instead of 234°F (soft ball stage adjusted)
- Subtract ~2°F per 1,000 feet elevation
- Using a candy thermometer is essential at altitude!

**Critical Success Factors:**
- All ingredients at room temperature for even cooking
- Stir constantly to prevent scorching
- Don't rush - medium heat, not high
- Temperature is more reliable than timing at altitude

**Storage:**
- Room temperature: 2 weeks in airtight container
- Refrigerated: 1 month
- Frozen: Up to 4 months
- Best texture at room temp (fridge makes it harder)

**Serving:**
- Cut into cubes, stack on plate
- Can be stored and served at room temperature
- Classic Christmas candy

**Christmas Timeline:**
- Tested at altitude with great results
