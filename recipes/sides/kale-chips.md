# Kale Chips

**Crispy, addictive, healthy snack**

- **Prep Time**: 10 minutes
- **Cook Time**: 20-25 minutes
- **Total Time**: 30-35 minutes
- **Yield**: 2-3 servings per bunch
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Type**: Snack/Side

## Ingredients

- [ ] **Kale** (1 bunch, about 3 stems)
- [ ] **Oil** (1-2 tsp - algae oil or olive oil)
- [ ] **Salt** (to taste)

### Optional Seasonings
- Garlic powder
- Nutritional yeast
- Smoked paprika
- Everything bagel seasoning

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep
1. Preheat oven to **300°F**
2. Wash kale thoroughly
3. Dry VERY well - use a salad spinner or pat with towels (moisture = soggy chips)
4. Remove tough stems
5. Tear leaves into chip-sized pieces (they shrink!)

### Step 2: Season
1. Place kale in large bowl
2. Drizzle with oil
3. Massage oil into leaves with your hands until evenly coated
4. Sprinkle with salt and any optional seasonings

### Step 3: Bake
1. Spread in a **single layer** on baking sheet(s) - don't overlap!
2. Bake 20-25 minutes, rotating pan halfway
3. Watch carefully at the end - they go from perfect to burnt quickly
4. Done when crispy but still green (not brown)

### Step 4: Cool
Let cool on pan for 5 minutes - they crisp up more as they cool.

## Notes & Tips

- **Dry kale is essential** - any moisture will steam instead of crisp
- **Don't overcrowd** - use multiple pans if needed
- **Low and slow** - higher temps burn before crisping
- **Season after if using nutritional yeast** - it can burn

## Storage

- Best eaten same day
- Store in paper bag at room temperature (not airtight - they'll get soggy)
- Will keep 2-3 days but lose crispness
