# Smoked Salmon Blini with Crème Fraîche & Dill
- **Prep Time**: 30 minutes (blini making)
- **Assembly Time**: 15 minutes
- **Total Time**: 45 minutes (can be split across 2 days)
- **Yield**: 12 mini blini canapés (3 per person for 4 guests)
- **Difficulty**: Medium (easy assembly, moderate blini technique)
- **Type**: Christmas Eve Appetizer / Canapé
- **Source**: Christmas Eve 2025 Menu, designed for make-ahead assembly

### Ingredients

#### Blini
- [ ] **Buckwheat blini mix** (Regiis Ova or similar)
- [ ] **Water or milk** (per package instructions) - *Location: upstairs fridge*
- [ ] **Neutral oil or butter** (for cooking) - *Location: downstairs or upstairs pantry*

#### Topping
- [ ] **High-quality smoked salmon** (3-4 oz, sliced not chunky)
- [ ] **Plain crème fraîche** (4-5 tbsp)
- [ ] **Fresh dill** (finely chopped)
- [ ] **Fresh chives** (finely sliced, optional)
- [ ] **Freshly ground black pepper** - *Location: upstairs pantry*

#### Optional Garnish
- [ ] **Salmon roe** (a few beads per blini)
- [ ] **Lemon zest** (very light touch)

### Equipment
- [ ] **Nonstick skillet or griddle**
- [ ] **Offset spatula**
- [ ] **Piping bag or small spoon**
- [ ] **Paper towels**
- [ ] **Serving platter** (chilled)

### Instructions

#### 1. Make the Blini (Can be done 24 hours ahead)
1. Prepare blini batter according to package instructions
2. Heat a lightly oiled nonstick skillet over medium-low heat
3. Spoon batter into small rounds (≈1½–2 inches diameter)
4. Cook until bubbles form and edges set; flip and cook briefly on second side
5. Transfer to a rack or paper towel to cool completely
6. **Make-ahead:** Store airtight in refrigerator for up to 24 hours

#### 2. Prepare the Salmon (Up to 12 hours ahead)
1. Cut smoked salmon into thin strips
2. Gently fold or roll into loose rosettes
3. Cover and refrigerate until assembly

#### 3. Assembly (Day Of - ≤30 minutes before serving)
1. Warm blini gently if desired (covered, low oven or skillet)
2. Pipe or spoon a small dollop of crème fraîche onto each blini
3. Top with a salmon rosette
4. Sprinkle lightly with dill and chives
5. Finish with black pepper
6. Optional: add 2–3 beads of salmon roe or a whisper of lemon zest
7. Arrange in a single layer on a chilled platter

### Notes

**Timing & Storage:**
- **Blini:** Make the day before or morning of
- **Salmon rosettes:** Prep up to 12 hours ahead
- **Final assembly:** ≤30 minutes before serving
- Best served slightly cool, not cold

**Plating:**
- Keep garnishing minimal — this canapé should feel clean and refined
- Arrange in single layer on chilled platter
- Works beautifully alongside caviar service as a first bite

**Variations:**
- Substitute crème fraîche with cultured cashew cream for dairy-free version
- Add a micro dill frond for visual lift instead of chopped herbs

**Pairing:**
- Best with **Blanc de Blancs Champagne**
- Perfect opener for Christmas Eve caviar service
