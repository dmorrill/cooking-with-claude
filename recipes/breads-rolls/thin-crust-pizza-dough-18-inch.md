# Thin Crust Pizza Dough (Two 18" Pizzas)

- **Prep Time**: 15 minutes
- **Rise Time**: 1-1.5 hours (first rise) + 30 min (second rise)
- **Total Time**: ~2 hours
- **Yield**: 2 large 18" thin crust pizzas
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Type**: Bread/Dough
- **Source**: Original recipe

## Ingredients

- [ ] **Warm water** (1½ cups, 105-110°F) - *not too hot or it kills the yeast*
- [ ] **Sugar** (1½ tsp) - *feeds the yeast*
- [ ] **Active dry yeast** (1 Tbsp / heaping 2¼ tsp) - *Location: upstairs fridge door*
- [ ] **Flour** (4½ cups, bread flour preferred, AP works) - *Location: pantry*
- [ ] **Salt** (1½ tsp) - *Location: upstairs pantry*
- [ ] **Olive oil** (3 Tbsp) - *Location: upstairs pantry*

## Equipment

- [ ] Large mixing bowl
- [ ] Kitchen towel or plastic wrap
- [ ] Floured surface for kneading
- [ ] Pizza stone or baking sheet

## Instructions

### 1. Bloom the Yeast (5 min)
1. Combine warm water + sugar + yeast in large bowl
2. Stir gently, let sit 5 minutes
3. Should get foamy - if not, yeast is dead, start over

### 2. Make the Dough (10 min)
1. Add flour, salt, and olive oil to yeast mixture
2. Stir until shaggy dough forms
3. Turn out onto floured surface
4. Knead 5-8 minutes until smooth and elastic
5. Dough should spring back when poked

### 3. First Rise (1-1.5 hours)
1. Oil the bowl
2. Place dough back in, turn to coat
3. Cover with towel
4. Let rise until doubled in size

### 4. Shape & Second Rise (30 min)
1. Punch down the dough
2. Divide into 2 equal pieces
3. Shape each into a smooth ball (tuck edges under)
4. Cover with towel, rest 30 minutes

### 5. Stretch & Bake
1. Preheat oven to 500-550°F with pizza stone for 45-60 min
2. On floured surface, stretch dough using knuckles
3. Work from center outward, let gravity help
4. Stretch all the way to edges (no puffy rim for thin crust)
5. Top and bake 6-8 minutes until bubbly and golden

## Notes

### Altitude Adjustments (Denver/5000+ ft)
- Dough may rise faster OR slower - go by size, not time
- **Refrigerate dough balls** after shaping to prevent over-proofing
- Pull from fridge 20-30 min before stretching
- Cold dough also stretches easier (less spring-back)

### Thin Crust Tips
- Stretch all the way to edges - no puffy rim
- Should be thin enough to almost see through in places
- Use knuckles to stretch from center outward
- Drape over fists and rotate, letting gravity help
- It's okay if it's not perfectly round!

### Make-Ahead Options
- Dough balls can refrigerate overnight (slow rise = more flavor)
- Pull out 30 min before stretching
- Can also freeze shaped balls up to 1 month

### Crispy Crust Tip
- Use Algae Cooking Oil instead of olive oil (higher smoke point)
- Make sure pizza stone is FULLY preheated (45-60 min)
- 550°F is better than 500°F if your oven goes that high
