## Meal Planning Workflows

### Scenario 1: "What can I cook tonight without shopping?"
**Steps:**
1. Check current time and available prep time
2. Review all inventory locations for available ingredients
3. Match against recipes database by prep time
4. Suggest 2-3 options with ingredient locations

### Scenario 2: "Plan meals for the week"
**Steps:**
1. Review current inventory across all locations
2. Identify ingredients that need to be used soon (expiring)
3. Suggest meals that use those ingredients first
4. Fill in remaining meals with family favorites
5. Generate shopping list for missing ingredients

### Scenario 3: "Use up ingredients before they expire"
**Steps:**
1. Check expiration dates in all inventory files
2. Prioritize recipes using items expiring soonest
3. Suggest batch cooking or preservation options
4. Create meal plan focused on these ingredients
