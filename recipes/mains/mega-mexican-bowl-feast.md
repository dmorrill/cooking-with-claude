# MEGA Mexican Bowl Feast
- **Prep Time**: 45 minutes
- **Cook Time**: 45 minutes
- **Total Time**: 1.5 hours
- **Yield**: 6-8 servings (meal prep friendly!)
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Type**: Complete Meal / Meal Prep
- **Created**: December 29, 2025

*Note: This epic Mexican feast was accidentally created when ALL the soyrizo spices went into the rice - making it incredibly flavorful but spicy! The normal version below has proper spice distribution.*

## Components Overview

This feast includes 5 main components that can be mixed and matched all week:
1. Mexican Rice (properly spiced this time!)
2. Mushroom-Enhanced Soyrizo
3. Beans (your choice)
4. Roasted Vegetables
5. Fresh Toppings & Sauces

## Component 1: Mexican Rice (Normal Version)

### Ingredients
- **2 cups brown rice** (or white rice for 20 min version)
- **3 Tbsp olive oil**
- **1/4 cup onion**, finely diced
- **3 cloves garlic**, minced
- **1/4 cup tomato paste**
- **4.5 cups vegetable broth** (3.5 cups for white rice)
- **1 tsp ground cumin**
- **1/2 tsp smoked paprika**
- **1/2 tsp chili powder**
- **1 tsp salt** (less if using salted broth)
- **1/4 tsp black pepper**
- **Juice from 1 lime** (at end)
- **1/4 cup cilantro**, chopped (at end)

### Instructions
1. Heat oil in large pot over medium-high heat
2. Add rice, stir constantly 5-7 minutes until golden and nutty
3. Add onion, cook 2 minutes; add garlic, cook 30 seconds
4. Add tomato paste, stir 1 minute until darkened
5. Add spices, stir 30 seconds
6. Pour in broth (will sizzle!), bring to boil
7. Reduce to low, cover, simmer 40-45 min for brown rice (18-20 for white)
8. Turn off heat, rest 5 minutes covered
9. Fluff with fork, add lime juice and cilantro

## Component 2: Mushroom-Enhanced Soyrizo (Scaled Recipe)

### Ingredients
- **28 oz extra firm tofu**, pressed and crumbled
- **306g mixed mushrooms**, finely minced in food processor
- **3.5 Tbsp olive oil**
- **5 cloves garlic**, minced
- **3.5 Tbsp apple cider vinegar**
- **1.75 Tbsp soy sauce**

**Spice Mix:**
- **3.5 Tbsp chili powder**
- **1.75 Tbsp smoked paprika**
- **3.5 tsp ground cumin**
- **1.75 tsp ground coriander**
- **1.75 tsp dried oregano**
- **Scant 1 tsp ground cinnamon** (essential!)
- **Heaping 1/4 tsp ground cloves**
- **1/4-1/2 tsp cayenne** (to taste)
- **1.75 tsp salt**
- **Scant 1 tsp black pepper**

### Instructions
1. Press tofu 20 minutes, then crumble into small pieces
2. Pulse mushrooms in food processor to fine mince (not puree)
3. Mix all spices in small bowl
4. Heat oil in large skillet over medium-high heat
5. Cook mushrooms 5-7 minutes until moisture evaporates
6. Add tofu, cook 5 minutes for golden edges
7. Add garlic and spice mix, cook 2 minutes until fragrant
8. Add vinegar and soy sauce, cook until absorbed
9. Continue cooking 5-10 minutes for crispy edges

## Component 3: Roasted Vegetables

### Ingredients
- **2 bell peppers**, sliced
- **1 large onion**, sliced
- **2-3 jalapeños**, whole or halved
- **2 Tbsp olive oil**
- **Salt and pepper**

### Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 425°F
2. Toss vegetables with oil, salt, pepper
3. Roast 25-30 minutes until caramelized edges

## Component 4: All The Toppings

### Fresh Prep
- **2 medium tomatoes**, diced
- **3 bunches cilantro**, chopped
- **2 jalapeños**, sliced thin
- **6 limes**, cut into wedges

### Sauces & Creamy Elements
- **Guacamole** (3-4 avocados)
- **Chipotle-Labneh Crema**: 1/2 cup labneh + 1-2 tsp adobo sauce + lime juice
- **Salsa seca** for crunch
- **Queso fresco**, crumbled

## Assembly - The Perfect Bowl

1. **Base**: 3/4 cup Mexican rice
2. **Proteins**: Create sections with soyrizo, roasted veggies, beans
3. **Cool Center**: Big dollop of guacamole
4. **Fresh Layer**: Tomatoes, cilantro
5. **Finishing**: Queso fresco, chipotle crema drizzle, salsa seca, lime wedge

- Fresh toppings: Keep separate, 3-4 days
- Makes 6-8 complete meals

## Pro Tips
- Toast rice until truly golden for best texture
- Press tofu well for crispy soyrizo
- Keep components separate until serving
- The cinnamon in soyrizo is KEY - don't skip!
- At Denver altitude: Add extra liquid and time
