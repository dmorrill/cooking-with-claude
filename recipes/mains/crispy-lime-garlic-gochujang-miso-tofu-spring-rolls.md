# Crispy Lime-Garlic Gochujang-Miso Tofu Spring Rolls

**Oven-roasted tofu with miso-gochujang-lime glaze, wrapped in double rice paper and pan-seared for maximum crackle**

- **Prep Time**: 35 minutes
- **Cook Time**: 25 minutes
- **Total Time**: ~60 minutes
- **Servings**: 3 substantial rolls
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Type**: Lunch (see notes)
- **Calories**: ~480-490 total

## Concept

Oven-roasted tofu (for moisture control and structure) finished with a miso-gochujang-lime glaze, wrapped generously with crisp vegetables in double rice-paper wrappers, then pan-seared for maximum crackle. Fewer, better rolls. Excellent dipping sauce. No leftovers.

## Ingredients

### Tofu
- [ ] **Extra-firm tofu** (1 block)
- [ ] **Algae oil** (neutral, high-heat) for roasting

### Gochujang-Miso Glaze
- [ ] **Soy sauce or tamari** (1.5 Tbsp)
- [ ] **White miso** (1 tsp)
- [ ] **Gochujang** (1 tsp)
- [ ] **Maple syrup or sugar** (1 tsp)
- [ ] **Garlic** (1 small clove, very finely grated)
- [ ] **Black Chinese vinegar** (0.5-1 tsp)
- [ ] **Lime** (1, juiced - divided)
- [ ] **Warm water** (1-2 tsp, to loosen if needed)

### Vegetables
- [ ] **Carrot** (1, julienned)
- [ ] **Bell pepper** (1, thinly sliced)
- [ ] **Cucumber** (0.5-1, cut into thin sticks)

### Other
- [ ] **Rice paper wrappers** (6 - for double-wrapped rolls)
- [ ] **Toasted sesame oil** (finish only)

### Dipping Sauce
- [ ] **Soy sauce or tamari** (1.5 Tbsp)
- [ ] **Lime juice** (from 0.5-1 lime)
- [ ] **Maple syrup** (1 tsp)
- [ ] **White miso** (0.5 tsp)
- [ ] **Gochujang** (0.5 tsp)
- [ ] **Garlic** (tiny bit, grated)
- [ ] **Toasted sesame oil** (1-2 drops, optional)

## Instructions

### Step 1: Make the Glaze
Whisk together:
- Soy/tamari
- White miso
- Gochujang
- Maple/sugar
- Garlic
- Black vinegar
- **Half the lime juice**
- Warm water if needed

Reserve half for finishing.

### Step 2: Marinate Tofu (10-20 min)
1. Pat tofu very dry
2. Cut into thin planks or batons (thin = better texture)
3. Toss tofu gently with **half the glaze**
4. Let sit while prepping vegetables
5. Before roasting, **shake off excess** - tofu should be coated, not wet

### Step 3: Prep Vegetables
- **Carrot**: Julienne, toss with pinch of salt + tiny squeeze lime, rest 5 min
- **Cucumber**: Toss with pinch of salt, let release moisture, pat dry
- **Bell pepper**: Slice thin, leave raw

Keep all filling components dry.

### Step 4: Oven-Roast Tofu
1. Heat oven to **425°F (220°C)**
2. Line sheet pan with parchment
3. Spread tofu in a single layer
4. Drizzle with **1 tsp algae oil**, toss lightly
5. Bake **20 minutes**, flipping once halfway
   - Goal is moisture reduction, not excessive browning

**Finish while hot:**
- Toss with remaining glaze
- Add remaining lime juice
- Add **1/4 tsp toasted sesame oil**

Let cool slightly.

### Step 5: Assemble Rolls (Double-Wrapped)
1. Dip **two rice paper wrappers at once** in warm water
2. Lay them slightly offset
3. Divide tofu into **3 equal piles**
4. Per roll, add:
   - 1/3 of the tofu
   - Compressed bundle of vegetables
5. Roll **tight and compact**
6. Place seam-side down and rest **5-7 minutes** to seal

### Step 6: Pan-Sear Rolls
1. Heat nonstick or well-seasoned pan over **medium-high**
2. Add **1 tsp algae oil total** (thin film)
3. Place rolls seam-side down
4. Do not move for ~90 seconds
5. Rotate to crisp all sides (5-6 min total)
6. Optional: return all rolls to pan together for **30 sec per side** to even crisp

### Dipping Sauce
Whisk all sauce ingredients until glossy. Adjust lime last.

## Serving
- Slice rolls **after frying**, on a deep bias
- Plate with space
- Sauce on the side

## Notes & Tips

### Timing Note
**Best served at lunch, not dinner.** Rice paper wrappers can cause blood sugar spikes - eating earlier in the day when insulin sensitivity is higher makes this easier to manage.

### Key Techniques
- **Double-wrap**: Two rice papers creates better structure and crispier result
- **Dry fillings**: Wet vegetables = soggy rolls. Salt and drain everything
- **Rest before searing**: 5-7 min rest lets wrappers seal properly
- **Don't move too soon**: Let bottom crisp before rotating

### Oil Choices
- **Algae oil**: Used for high-heat stability and reduced AGE risk
- **Toasted sesame oil**: Used only as aromatic finish (not for cooking)

### Flavor Balance
Flavor should taste slightly intense on tofu alone - vegetables and wrapper balance it.

## Storage
- Best eaten immediately
- Leftovers can be refrigerated but will lose crispiness
- Reheat in dry pan to restore some texture

## Recipe Origin
Developed as a healthier, vegan spring roll with intentional portion control and maximum flavor. This is the canonical "3-roll version" - fewer, better, stop when excellence ends.
