# Asian Crispy Tofu Bowl with Spaghetti Squash

**Healthy, flavor-packed bowl using pantry staples - perfect for using up vegetables**

- **Prep Time**: 15 minutes (plus all-day marinating)
- **Cook Time**: 45 minutes
- **Total Time**: 1 hour (plus marinating)
- **Servings**: 2
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Type**: Dinner, Weeknight, Healthy

## Why This Recipe Works

This is a go-to for using up common fridge staples: tofu, broccoli, bell peppers, and beans. The all-day marinade transforms basic tofu into something special, and spaghetti squash makes it low-carb and filling. The peanut drizzle + shiitake oil combo is the secret weapon.

## Ingredients

### For the All-Day Marinade
- 1 block (14 oz) extra-firm tofu, pressed and cubed
- 3 Tbsp soy sauce
- 1 Tbsp gochujang
- 1 tsp white miso
- 1 Tbsp sesame oil
- 1 Tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp maple syrup
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated

### For the Bowl
- 1 spaghetti squash (medium)
- 1 head broccoli, cut into florets
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 cup cooked black beans (seasoned with cumin/garlic/onion works great)
- 1-2 cloves garlic, minced (for stir-fry)
- 1 Tbsp cooking oil (for squash)
- 1 Tbsp cooking oil (for stir-fry)
- Salt to taste

### For the Peanut Drizzle
- 2 Tbsp peanut butter
- 1 Tbsp soy sauce
- 1 Tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp maple syrup
- 2 Tbsp warm water

### For Finishing
- Algae Shiitake Mushroom Oil (or regular sesame oil)

## Instructions

### Morning: Marinate the Tofu
1. **Press tofu**: Wrap in clean towels, place weight on top for 10-15 minutes
2. **Cut into 1-inch cubes**
3. **Mix marinade**: Whisk together soy sauce, gochujang, miso, sesame oil, rice vinegar, maple syrup, garlic, and ginger
4. **Combine**: Place tofu in container, pour marinade over, gently toss
5. **Refrigerate**: Marinate all day (6-8 hours ideal), flip once if you remember

### Evening: Cook (~45 min)

**5:00** - Preheat oven to 400°F

**5:05** - Prep spaghetti squash:
- Cut in half lengthwise (careful - use a sturdy knife)
- Scoop out seeds with a spoon
- Brush cut sides with oil, sprinkle with salt

**5:10** - Place squash CUT-SIDE DOWN on sheet pan, roast 40 minutes

**5:35** - Prep and roast tofu:
- Line second sheet pan with parchment
- Remove tofu from marinade (SAVE the marinade)
- Spread cubes in single layer, don't crowd
- Bake 25-30 minutes, flip halfway

**5:45** - Make peanut sauce:
- Whisk all peanut drizzle ingredients until smooth
- Set aside

**5:50** - Prep vegetables and flip tofu:
- Cut broccoli into bite-size florets
- Slice bell pepper into strips
- Mince garlic

**5:55** - Check squash (should be fork-tender) and stir-fry:
- Flip squash cut-side up
- Heat oil in large skillet over HIGH heat
- Add garlic, stir 30 seconds
- Add broccoli and peppers, stir-fry 5-6 minutes until crisp-tender

**6:00** - Finish components:
- Warm black beans (microwave or stovetop), add splash of reserved marinade
- Scrape spaghetti squash into strands with a fork

**6:05** - Assemble bowls!

## Assembly

In each bowl, layer:
1. Spaghetti squash strands (base)
2. Crispy baked tofu
3. Stir-fried broccoli and bell peppers
4. Seasoned black beans
5. Drizzle with peanut sauce
6. Finish with Algae Shiitake Mushroom Oil (be generous!)

## Notes & Tips

- **Squash not tender?** Give it 5-10 more minutes
- **Tofu not crispy enough?** Broil 2-3 minutes at the end (watch closely)
- **Veggies getting soggy?** Heat is too low - crank it up
- **The shiitake oil is non-negotiable** - it adds incredible umami depth

## Storage & Make-Ahead

- **Marinade ahead**: Tofu can marinate 6-24 hours
- **Squash ahead**: Roast squash up to 2 days ahead, reheat in oven
- **Leftover storage**: Components keep 3-4 days refrigerated
- **Reheating**: Tofu reheats best in oven/air fryer to restore crispiness

## Variations

### Base Swaps
- **Rice**: Use jasmine or brown rice (start at 5:00, simmer while squash roasts)
- **Noodles**: Rice noodles or soba work great
- **Cauliflower rice**: Quick sauté at the end

### Vegetable Swaps
- **Snap peas + carrots**: Classic stir-fry combo
- **Bok choy + mushrooms**: Add mushrooms first (they need longer)
- **Zucchini + green beans**: Cut to similar sizes

### Protein Swaps
- **Tempeh**: Slice into strips, marinate same way
- **Edamame**: Add to bowl as additional protein

### Bean Variations
- Black beans with cumin/garlic/onion (as written)
- Chickpeas with curry powder
- White beans with Italian herbs
- Skip beans entirely for lower-carb
