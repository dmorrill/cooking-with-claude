# Braised Goat Leg Steaks

**Prep Time:** 20 minutes
**Cook Time:** 3–3.5 hours
**Servings:** 4–6 (as a topping bar component; 2–3 as a main)
**Difficulty:** Easy (mostly hands-off)

Rich, tender, pull-apart goat with a clean herbal jus. Savory, not heavy — brightened with lemon and fresh herbs. Excellent over mashed potatoes. Great as part of a mashed potato bar.

---

## Ingredients

### Meat
- 2 goat leg steaks, bone-in (~1 lb each)
- Salt + freshly ground black pepper
- 1–2 Tbsp high-heat oil (for searing)

### Aromatics
- 1 onion, sliced or chunked
- 3–5 garlic cloves, smashed
- 1 carrot, chunked (optional)
- 1 celery stalk, chunked (optional)

### Liquid
- ~1 cup red wine
- ~1 cup water, stock, or vegetable broth (combined should come ½–⅔ up the meat)

### Herbs
- 1–2 bay leaves
- 2–3 sprigs fresh thyme or rosemary

### Finish (do not skip)
- Zest of 1 lemon
- Fresh lemon juice, to taste
- Handful of fresh parsley, chopped (or chives)

---

## Method

### 1. Sear
- Pat meat completely dry, season generously on all sides with salt and pepper
- Heat oil in a Dutch oven over high heat until shimmering
- Sear on all sides until deeply browned, 3–4 min per side
- Remove and set aside

### 2. Build the Braise
- In the same pot, reduce heat to medium
- Add onion, cook until softened, ~5 min
- Add garlic (and carrot/celery if using), cook 1–2 min
- Return meat to pot
- Add wine, then stock — liquid should come ½–⅔ up the sides of the meat (not submerged)
- Tuck in bay leaves and herb sprigs
- Bring to a gentle simmer

### 3. Braise
- Cover and transfer to oven at **300–325°F**
- Cook **2.5–3.5 hours** until meat is fork-tender and pulling away from the bone
- Check once midway — liquid should be at a lazy simmer, not boiling

### 4. Finish
- Remove meat from pot, pull off bone, shred or break into chunks
- Skim excess fat from braising liquid
- Simmer liquid over medium heat until slightly reduced and glossy
- Return meat to the jus
- Right before serving: stir in lemon zest, a squeeze of lemon juice, and chopped parsley

---

## Notes

**Flavor profile:** Clean and savory — no tomato, no sweetness. The brightness comes entirely from the lemon finish. Don't skip it.

**Make-ahead:** This dish is better after resting. Braise in the morning, let it sit, reheat gently before serving. The jus thickens and the flavor deepens.

**Serving:** Spoon over mashed potatoes and let the jus soak in. Works beautifully as a topping bar component alongside caramelized onions, sautéed mushrooms, caviar, crème fraîche, and fresh herbs.

**Quantity note:** 2 bone-in steaks (~2 lbs) yields roughly 1–1.5 lbs of meat after braising — enough for 4–6 as a topping, 2–3 as a plated main.
