# Mirin-Miso-Gochujang Roasted Tofu

**Umami-bomb roasted tofu with five-sauce marinade**

- **Prep Time**: 10 minutes (plus 15+ min pressing)
- **Cook Time**: 25-30 minutes
- **Total Time**: 50+ minutes
- **Servings**: 2
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Type**: Dinner, Weeknight, Healthy

## Ingredients

### For the Marinade
- 1 block (14-16 oz) extra-firm tofu, pressed and cubed
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 1 tbsp miso paste
- 1 tbsp gochujang
- 1 tsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- ½ tsp ground ginger (or 1 inch fresh, grated)
- 2-3 cloves garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. **Press tofu**: Wrap in clean towels, place heavy object on top for 15+ minutes
2. **Cut into 1-inch cubes**
3. **Make marinade**: Whisk together soy sauce, mirin, miso, gochujang, fish sauce, sesame oil, rice vinegar, ginger, and garlic
4. **Marinate**: Coat tofu with marinade, let sit at least 15-30 minutes (longer is better)
5. **Preheat oven** to 400-425°F, line sheet pan with parchment
6. **Arrange tofu**: Single layer, space between pieces, drain excess marinade
7. **Roast**: 25-30 minutes, flip halfway through
8. **Optional crisp**: Broil last 2-3 minutes for extra crispy edges

## Serving Suggestion (Tested)

Roast alongside on a separate sheet pan:
- 1 can chickpeas (drained)
- Butternut squash, cubed
- Broccoli florets
- Cauliflower florets
- Bell peppers, chopped
- Toss in leftover marinara sauce + olive oil + turmeric
- Same oven temp, same timing

This combo covers beans, cruciferous vegetables, other vegetables, herbs/spices, and plant protein in one meal.

## Notes

- **First made**: April 2026
- **Ground ginger sub works fine** — fresh ginger's volatile aromatics cook off in the oven anyway
- **Fish sauce**: 1 tsp is enough — adds depth without fishiness
- **Five sauces** (soy, mirin, miso, gochujang, fish sauce) each bring something different: salt, sweet glaze, umami body, sweet heat, funky depth

## Balanced Plate
- Pair with beans, greens, and whole grains for a well-rounded plate

## Storage
- Leftovers keep 3-4 days refrigerated
- Reheat in oven/air fryer to restore crispiness
