# Spicy Chorizo Mayo
- **Prep Time**: 5 minutes
- **Cook Time**: None
- **Total Time**: 5 minutes
- **Yield**: About 2/3 cup
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Type**: Condiment / Sauce
- **Created**: December 2024

### Ingredients

- [ ] **Japanese mayo (Kewpie)** (1/2 cup)
- [ ] **Homemade soyrizo** (2-3 Tbsp, cooled)
- [ ] **Lime juice** (1 tsp)
- [ ] **Smoked paprika** (1/2 tsp)
- [ ] **Cayenne pepper** (1/4 tsp, or to taste)
- [ ] **Chipotle in adobo** (1 tsp, optional for smoky heat)

### Instructions

1. Make soyrizo first and let cool slightly
2. Reserve 2-3 Tbsp of the cooked soyrizo
3. In small bowl, mix Japanese mayo with cooled soyrizo
4. Add lime juice, smoked paprika, and cayenne
5. Add chipotle in adobo if using
6. Mix well until uniform color
7. Taste and adjust heat level
8. Transfer to jar or squeeze bottle

### Storage & Usage

**Storage:**
- Refrigerator: 1 week in airtight container
- Not suitable for freezing

**Perfect for:**
- Drizzling on Mexican bowls
- Spreading in quesadillas and burritos
- Dipping sauce for roasted vegetables
- Burger or sandwich spread
- Mix into deviled eggs
- Toss with roasted potatoes
- Spread on corn on the cob

### Notes
- **Japanese mayo advantage**: Richer, creamier than regular mayo due to egg yolks and rice vinegar
- **Soyrizo temperature**: Let cool so it doesn't thin the mayo
- **Heat customization**: Start with less cayenne, can always add more
- **Texture option**: Blend for smoother sauce or leave chunky
- **Partner's favorite**: Makes Mexican bowls restaurant-quality
- **Make-ahead**: Flavors meld and improve after a few hours
- **Variation**: Use real chorizo for non-vegetarian version
