# Horseradish Crème Fraîche
- **Prep Time**: 5 minutes (plus 1 hour chill)
- **Total Time**: 65 minutes
- **Yield**: 1 cup (enough for 4-6 servings)
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Type**: Condiment / Sauce
- **Source**: Christmas 2025 Menu

### Ingredients
- [ ] **Plain crème fraîche** (1 cup) - *Location: order from Whole Foods*
- [ ] **Prepared horseradish** (1-2 tbsp, to taste) - *Location: upstairs fridge*
- [ ] **Fine salt** (pinch) - *Location: upstairs pantry*
- [ ] **Black pepper** (freshly ground) - *Location: upstairs pantry*
- [ ] **Lemon** (squeeze, optional) - *Location: order from Whole Foods*

### Equipment
- [ ] **Small mixing bowl**
- [ ] **Spoon**
- [ ] **Measuring spoons**

### Instructions

#### 1. Mix
1. Combine crème fraîche, horseradish, salt, and pepper in bowl
2. Stir until well combined
3. Taste and adjust seasoning
4. Add optional lemon juice if desired (brightens flavor)

#### 2. Chill
1. Cover and refrigerate at least 1 hour before serving
2. Flavors will meld and improve with time

### Notes

**Flavor Balance:**
- Start with 1 tbsp horseradish and taste - add more for stronger kick
- Should be tangy and sharp but not overwhelming
- Fresh crème fraîche is creamier than sour cream

**Storage:**
- Keeps 3-4 days refrigerated in airtight container
- Flavor intensifies slightly over time

**Make-Ahead:**
- Best made at least 1 hour ahead (up to 3 days)
- Can be made 1-3 days ahead

**Serving:**
- Essential with roast beef and Yorkshire puddings
- Also excellent on sandwiches (12/24 lunch)
- Room temperature or slightly chilled

**Variations:**
- Add fresh dill for herb version
- Substitute Greek yogurt for lighter option
- Use wasabi instead of horseradish for different heat profile
