# Whipped Tofu Miso Dip with Cashew Cream, Caramelized Onions & Herbs

- **Prep Time**: 15 minutes active (plus cashew soaking)
- **Cook Time**: 30-40 minutes (caramelizing onions)
- **Total Time**: ~1 hour
- **Yield**: ~2 cups (enough for 6-8 servings)
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Type**: Vegan Dip / Crudités / Game Day
- **Source**: Original recipe

## Ingredients

### Base
- [ ] **Extra-firm tofu** (1 block, ~14 oz) - *Location: check fridge*
- [ ] **Raw cashews** (1 cup) - *Location: pantry*
- [ ] **Roasted cauliflower** (1 cup, leftover) - *Location: fridge*
- [ ] **White miso** (2-3 Tbsp) - *Location: upstairs fridge door*
- [ ] **Garlic** (1-2 cloves) - *Location: upstairs pantry counter*
- [ ] **Lemon juice** (2 Tbsp) - *Location: upstairs pantry - fresh lemons*
- [ ] **Olive oil** (2 Tbsp) - *Location: upstairs pantry*
- [ ] **Water** (as needed for blending)
- [ ] **Salt** (to taste) - *Location: upstairs pantry*

### Fold-Ins
- [ ] **Yellow or white onion** (1-2 large, for caramelizing) - *Location: upstairs pantry*
- [ ] **Fresh herbs, chopped** (parsley, chives, dill - whatever you have) - *Location: upstairs fridge*

## Equipment
- [ ] **High-speed blender or food processor**
- [ ] **Large skillet** (for caramelizing onions)
- [ ] **Mixing bowl**
- [ ] **Spatula**

## Instructions

### 1. Soak Cashews
Choose your method:
- **Overnight**: Cover with water, refrigerate
- **2-4 hours**: Cover with hot water
- **Quick (15 min)**: Boil in water, then drain

### 2. Caramelize Onions (30-40 min)
1. Slice onions into thin half-moons
2. Heat a splash of oil in a large skillet over medium-low heat
3. Add onions, stir to coat
4. Cook low and slow, stirring occasionally
5. Add pinches of salt to help release moisture
6. Done when deep golden brown and jammy
7. Set aside to cool

### 3. Blend the Base
1. Drain soaked cashews
2. Add cashews to blender with ~½ cup water
3. Blend until completely smooth and creamy
4. Add tofu, roasted cauliflower, miso, garlic, lemon juice, and olive oil
5. **Add caramelized onions to the blender** (see note below)
6. Blend until silky smooth
7. Add more water if needed for desired consistency
8. Optional: Add 1 tsp rice wine vinegar or apple cider vinegar for extra brightness
9. Taste and adjust - more miso for umami, lemon/vinegar for brightness, salt as needed

### 4. Finish & Season
1. Transfer blended dip to a mixing bowl
2. Fold in chopped fresh herbs (chives work great!)
3. Taste again and adjust seasoning

### 5. Chill
Refrigerate at least 1 hour, preferably overnight. Flavors meld and texture firms up.

## Notes

**IMPORTANT - Blend the Onions!**
- Do NOT fold in the caramelized onions by hand - they get stringy and weird when dipping
- Blend the onions INTO the dip for smooth, even flavor
- Only fold in the herbs at the end for freshness

**Texture Goal:**
- Silky smooth throughout (no stringy bits)
- Should be scoopable but not stiff
- Extra-firm tofu needs more liquid than silken - adjust as you blend

**Make-Ahead:**
- This dip is BETTER the next day
- Make Friday night, serve Saturday
- Keeps 4-5 days refrigerated

**Flavor Balance:**
- Miso brings umami depth
- Roasted cauliflower adds subtle sweetness
- Caramelized onions bring richness
- Lemon keeps it bright
- Splash of vinegar (rice wine or ACV) adds extra tang
- Chives add mild onion freshness

**Serving Suggestions:**
- Crudités platter (broccoli, carrots, celery, cauliflower)
- Pita chips or crackers
- Spread on sandwiches or wraps
- Game day snacking

**Variations:**
- Add roasted garlic instead of raw for mellower flavor
- Stir in a spoonful of gochujang for heat
- Top with everything bagel seasoning
- Drizzle with chili oil before serving

**Storage:**
- Keeps 4-5 days refrigerated in airtight container
- Flavor improves over time
- Bring to room temp 15 min before serving for best texture

**Blender Tips:**
- This thick mixture can overheat a Vitamix - blend in short bursts
- If you get an error code (E3, ES2), unplug and let cool 15-20 min
- Add extra water to help it blend more easily
- Food processor works as backup
