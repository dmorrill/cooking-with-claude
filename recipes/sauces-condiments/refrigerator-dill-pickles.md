# Refrigerator Dill Pickles (Quick / No-Can)

**Source:** Original recipe · **Makes:** ~1 quart jar · **Difficulty:** Easy · **Active:** ~15 min + 24h+ chill
Crisp, garlicky-dill fridge pickles. No canning — they live in the fridge and are ready to snack in a day.

## Ingredients
- ~1 lb **cucumbers** — English + baby seedless work great (baby = extra crunch), sliced into spears or ¼-inch coins
- 3–4 sprigs fresh **dill** (or 1–2 tsp dill seed)
- 3–4 **garlic cloves**, smashed
- **Brine:**
  - 1 cup water
  - 1 cup **apple cider vinegar** *(or distilled white for a cleaner/classic taste)*
  - 1 Tbsp salt
  - 1 tsp sugar (optional, rounds the tang)
- Optional spices: 1 tsp black peppercorns · ½ tsp coriander seeds · pinch red pepper flakes · mustard seed if you have it

## Method
1. **Pack** the cut cucumbers tightly into a clean quart jar with the dill, garlic, and any whole spices.
2. **Brine:** combine water, vinegar, salt, sugar in a saucepan; warm just until the salt/sugar dissolve (don't boil hard). Or shake cold in a jar until dissolved.
3. **Pour** the brine over to fully cover the cucumbers.
4. **Cool** to room temp, lid, and **refrigerate.**
5. **Ready in ~24h**, best flavor at **2–3 days.** Keep refrigerated up to ~2–3 weeks. *Not shelf-stable — fridge only.*

## Tips
- **Crunch:** keep them cold, slice fresh, and don't over-warm the brine. A grape/oak leaf or tiny pinch of black tea (tannins) keeps them extra crisp — optional.
- **Uses:** topping for hummus and bean-dip mezze platters, burger topping, snacking, chopped into a quick relish.
- **Why keep a jar around:** whole-food, plant-based, low-calorie, and a bright tangy counterpoint to rich dishes.
