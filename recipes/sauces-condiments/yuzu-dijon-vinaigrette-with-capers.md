# Yuzu-Dijon Vinaigrette with Capers

**Prep Time:** 5 minutes
**Makes:** About 1/2 cup
**Difficulty:** Easy

A bright, tangy, briny dressing made in a mortar and pestle. Perfect for cutting through rich dishes. The pounding creates a more emulsified dressing than whisking, and the shallot and capers become part of the dressing rather than just sitting in it.

## Ingredients

- 1 small shallot, roughly chopped
- 2 Tbsp capers, drained
- Pinch of salt
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 2-3 Tbsp yuzu persimmon vinegar (or other bright vinegar)
- 4-6 Tbsp olive oil

## Method (Mortar and Pestle)

1. **Pound the shallot:** Add shallot and pinch of salt to mortar. Pound into a paste - salt acts as abrasive.

2. **Add capers:** Add capers and pound to break them up, mixing into shallot paste.

3. **Add acid and mustard:** Add Dijon and yuzu vinegar. Stir with pestle to combine.

4. **Emulsify with oil:** Drizzle oil slowly while stirring with pestle. Dressing will become creamy and emulsified.

5. **Taste and adjust:** Add more vinegar for brightness, more oil to mellow, salt as needed.

## Serving Suggestions

- Arugula and spring greens (excellent with rich mains like ragu)
- Any salad that needs to balance a heavy dish
- Drizzled over roasted vegetables

## Presentation Tip
Make it at the table right before dressing the salad - the mortar and pestle makes for a great presentation.

## Variations
- Swap yuzu vinegar for sherry vinegar or red wine vinegar
- Add a small garlic clove with the shallot
- Finish with fresh herbs (chives work well here)
