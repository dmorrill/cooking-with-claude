# Vegetable Broth 2.0
- **Prep Time**: 15 minutes
- **Cook Time**: 1.5 hours (mostly passive simmering)
- **Total Time**: ~2 hours
- **Yield**: ~12 cups broth
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Type**: Base/Staple
- **Source**: Dr. Michael Greger & Robin Robertson, The How Not to Age Cookbook
- **Original Recipe**: https://nutritionfacts.org/recipe/vegetable-broth-2/

### Description
This all-purpose, salt-free broth can be used in any recipe calling for any kind of broth. It brings more flavor (and nutrients) to your cooking. The miso adds umami depth without salt.

### Ingredients
- [ ] **Red onion** (1 large, coarsely chopped)
- [ ] **Carrots** (2 large, cut into 1-inch pieces)
- [ ] **Celery** (2 large ribs, coarsely chopped)
- [ ] **Red bell pepper** (1 large, cored, seeded, cut into 2-inch pieces)
- [ ] **Garlic** (3 cloves, crushed)
- [ ] **Roma tomatoes** (2, cored and halved)
- [ ] **Dried shiitake mushrooms** (2)
- [ ] **Bay leaves** (2, optional)
- [ ] **Kombu** (1 2-inch piece dried sea vegetable, optional)
- [ ] **Fresh parsley** (½ cup coarsely chopped)
- [ ] **Ground pippali or black pepper** (½ teaspoon)
- [ ] **Water** (12 cups)
- [ ] **White miso paste** (2½ tablespoons)

### Equipment
- [ ] Large pot (at least 4 quarts)
- [ ] High-powered blender
- [ ] Fine-mesh sieve (optional, for straining)
- [ ] Storage containers with tight-fitting lids

### Instructions

#### Step 1: Combine & Simmer (1.5 hours)
1. In a large pot, combine the red onion, carrots, celery, bell pepper, and garlic
2. Stir in the tomatoes, mushrooms, bay leaves (if using), kombu (if using), parsley, and pippali/pepper
3. Add the 12 cups water and bring to a boil
4. Lower heat to low and simmer for 1½ hours
5. Remove from heat, let cool slightly
6. Remove and discard the bay leaves and kombu (if used)

#### Step 2: Blend & Season
7. Transfer the broth to a high-powered blender and blend until smooth
8. (Optional) Strain the blended broth through a fine-mesh sieve back into the pot, pressing vegetables against the sieve to release their juices
9. Ladle a few tablespoons of broth into a small bowl
10. Add the miso paste and stir well until dissolved
11. Incorporate the miso mixture back into the broth

#### Step 3: Cool & Store
12. Let the broth cool to room temperature
13. Divide into containers with tight-fitting lids
14. Label with date

### Storage
- **Refrigerator**: Up to 5 days
- **Freezer**: Up to 3 months

### Notes
- **Salt-free**: The miso provides umami and depth without added salt
- **Blending**: Blending the vegetables into the broth adds body and nutrients
- **Straining optional**: Skip straining for more nutrition, strain for clearer broth
- **Kombu**: Adds minerals and umami; available at Asian grocery stores or Whole Foods
- **Pippali**: A long pepper with warming properties; black pepper is a fine substitute
- **Freezer portions**: Consider freezing in 2-cup portions for easy use in recipes
- **Use for**: Soups, stews, cooking grains, deglazing pans, anywhere you need broth

### Why This Recipe
From the How Not to Age Cookbook - designed to maximize nutrition and flavor without added sodium. The blended vegetables add more nutrients than traditional strained broths.
