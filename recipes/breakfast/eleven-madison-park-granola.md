# Eleven Madison Park Granola

**Crunchy, aromatic granola with pistachios, coconut, and warm spices**

- **Prep Time**: 10 minutes
- **Cook Time**: 35-40 minutes
- **Total Time**: 50 minutes
- **Servings**: ~6 cups
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Type**: Breakfast / Snack
- **Tags**: In Rotation, Plant-based, Vegan

## Ingredients

### Dry Mix
- 2 3/4 cups high-protein rolled oats
- 1 cup shelled pistachios (unsalted)
- 1 cup unsweetened coconut chips
- 1/3 cup pumpkin seeds
- 1 tablespoon kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon cardamom
- 1 teaspoon nutmeg
- 2 tablespoons chia seeds
- 2 tablespoons ground flax seed

### Wet Mix
- 1/2 cup light brown sugar
- 1/3 cup maple syrup
- 1/3 cup olive oil

## Instructions

1. **Preheat oven** to 300°F
2. **Make wet mix**: Combine the brown sugar, maple syrup, and olive oil in a saucepan on low heat, stirring occasionally until the sugar has dissolved
3. **Make dry mix**: In a large bowl, mix together the oats, pistachios, coconut chips, pumpkin seeds, salt, nutmeg, cardamom, chia seeds, and ground flax seeds
4. **Combine**: Pour wet mix over dry mix and stir until evenly coated
5. **Spread**: Spread the mixture on a large baking sheet lined with parchment paper or a silicone baking mat
6. **Bake**: 35-40 minutes at 300°F, stirring occasionally
7. **Cool and store**: Let cool completely on the pan, then transfer to an airtight container

## Storage
- Airtight container at room temperature
- Stays good for 1-2 weeks

## Notes & Tips
- Low oven temp (300°F) is key — keeps it crunchy without burning
- Don't stir too often if you want bigger clusters
- The olive oil gives it a slightly savory edge that works beautifully with the cardamom
